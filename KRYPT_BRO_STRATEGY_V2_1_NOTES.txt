KRYPT BRO STRATEGY V2.1 - DELTA CRYPTO OPTIONS POLISH
Date: 2026-09-10

SCOPE
- BTC / ETH / GOLD only.
- Delta Exchange India only.
- Existing app.py, scanner lifecycle, Telegram delivery, dashboard, trading runtime,
  WebSocket modules and deployment structure were NOT redesigned.
- Strategy changes are isolated to backend/signal_engine.py.
- Original backend signal engine preserved as backend/signal_engine.py.original_v3.

ADDED / IMPROVED
1. 4H trend confirmation (EMA20/EMA50) as a SOFT filter, not a hard blocker.
2. 4H Fibonacci pivots in addition to Daily and 5M pivots.
3. Recent 15M swing Fib: 0.382 / 0.500 / 0.618 / 0.786.
4. Williams %R on 15M and 5M, used as timing confirmation.
5. ADX + +DI/-DI on 15M, used as trend-strength confirmation.
6. Volume score now also respects latest candle direction.
7. Direction-aware Fib score replaces neutral Fib scoring.
8. Counter-1H setups remain possible but receive a score penalty.
9. Delta option-chain enrichment after a fully confirmed underlying signal only.
10. Option selector prefers nearest valid expiry, near-ATM/slightly ITM contracts,
    useful Delta range, tighter spread, OI and volume.
11. Telegram signal can show selected Delta CALL/PUT, strike, expiry, current
    premium, mark price, Delta, OI, volume, spread and estimated premium SL/T1/T2/T3.

IMPORTANT OPTION NOTE
- Premium SL/T1/T2/T3 are local Greek (Delta/Gamma) estimates around the current
  market state. Actual premium can differ because IV, theta, liquidity and
  order-book conditions change.
- Option-chain failure NEVER blocks the core underlying signal scanner.
